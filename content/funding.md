IQSS supports affiliated faculty, graduate students, and undergraduates through various funding opportunities designed to advance social science knowledge.

## For Faculty

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../research-resources/rapidpeer/">rapidPeer (Magaro Peer Pre-Review)</a></h3>
<p>A peer pre-review similar to journal peer review, but faster, more complete, and constructive. Reviewers read the full paper and offer constructive suggestions throughout.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../research-resources/undergraduate-research-scholars/">Undergraduate Research Scholars Program</a></h3>
<p>An opportunity for Harvard College students to gain firsthand research experience working with faculty members across the University. Faculty receive up to $1,250/year in funding.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../research-resources/ecee/">Extraordinary Claims, Extraordinary Evidence (ECEE)</a></h3>
<p>The ECEE program empowers Harvard faculty through intensive support from IQSS to produce social science research that shifts the dominant scientific community position.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../funding-mindich/">Mindich Encounters with Authors Conference Series</a></h3>
<p>An opportunity for one or more authors to present a major work still in progress. Up to $10,000 in conference support. Rolling deadline.</p>
</div>
</div>

## For Graduate Students

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../funding-research-grants/">Research Grants</a></h3>
<p>Harvard graduate student affiliates currently enrolled in a doctoral program may apply for up to $3,000 to support dissertation research activities.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../funding-dissertation-awards/">Dissertation Awards</a></h3>
<p>The Henry A. Murray Research Archive offers the $2,500 Jeanne Humphrey Block Dissertation Award to a qualified Ph.D. candidate in the social sciences each spring.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3><a href="../funding-travel-grants/">Travel Grants</a></h3>
<p>Up to $1,000 for IQSS graduate student affiliates enrolled in a doctoral program, supporting travel for conference presentations. Rolling deadline.</p>
</div>
</div>
