Harvard University has been ranked **#1 in the world** for social-sciences research in the inaugural Nature Index 2025 social-sciences ranking, published July 15, 2026. Harvard achieved an article Share of **68.61** — more than 16 points ahead of second-ranked Stanford University (52.04) — out of a field that includes institutions from across the globe.

The Nature Index, published by *Nature*, is one of the most authoritative measures of high-quality research output, tracking articles published in a curated set of leading peer-reviewed journals. Social sciences was added to the Index for the first time in June 2025, encompassing fields including economics, political science, sociology, anthropology, law, and education.

## What Is the Share Metric?

The Nature Index uses **Share** as its primary measure of research output. Share is a fractional count that assigns credit for each article proportionally among its authors and their affiliated institutions. For example, if a paper has four authors from four different institutions, each institution receives a Share of 0.25 for that article. This approach ensures that credit is distributed fairly regardless of how many collaborators contributed, rewarding genuine intellectual participation rather than simply counting how many papers mention an institution's name.

A higher Share indicates that an institution's researchers are not just affiliated with published papers, but are active, central contributors to them. Harvard's Share of 68.61 — accumulated across 193 articles published in Nature Index social-sciences journals during 2025 — reflects the depth and frequency with which Harvard scholars are driving original social science research at the highest levels.

## IQSS Cited as a Key Factor

The Nature Index article specifically names the Institute for Quantitative Social Science as a facility that supports Harvard's world-leading social sciences output:

> *"Facilities that support Harvard's world-leading output in the social sciences include the Institute for Quantitative Social Science, which manages the Henry A. Murray Research Archive — a repository of more than 100 terabytes of data, audio and video collected from social-science research projects. Researchers from across the world can access the archive."*

The Henry A. Murray Research Archive, managed by IQSS, provides open access to longitudinal datasets, qualitative recordings, and research materials spanning decades of social science inquiry. Its scale and accessibility make it a unique resource for scholars worldwide and a significant contributor to the volume and quality of research Harvard produces.

## The IQSS Community Behind the Ranking

Rankings like this do not happen by accident — they are the product of a large, diverse, and deeply talented community of people working every day to push the boundaries of social science knowledge. The IQSS community includes:

- **Faculty affiliates** from nearly every school within Harvard University — the Faculty of Arts and Sciences, Harvard Kennedy School, Harvard Business School, Harvard Law School, Harvard Medical School, and more — who bring their research through IQSS and draw on its resources, data infrastructure, and collaborative networks
- **Graduate student affiliates** from across Harvard's doctoral programs, many of whom conduct their dissertation research with IQSS support, access IQSS funding, and participate in working groups and methods training
- **Postdoctoral fellows and visiting scholars** who come to IQSS from institutions around the world, enriching the intellectual environment and forming international research collaborations that amplify Harvard's global research footprint
- **Research staff and data scientists** in Data Science Services (DSS) who provide high-touch methodological consulting, helping researchers across the University design studies, analyze complex datasets, and publish at the highest levels
- **IT and research computing professionals** who maintain the infrastructure — from high-performance computing access to Dataverse to the Murray Archive — that makes large-scale, reproducible, data-intensive social science possible
- **Administrative staff** who support the funding programs, event series, sponsored research services, and affiliate community that keep IQSS functioning as a hub of scholarship

The Nature Index ranking captures the output of this entire ecosystem. Every paper counted in Harvard's Share of 68.61 represents researchers who were supported, connected, or enabled in some way by the resources IQSS provides and the community it sustains.

Harvard's Share of 68.61 represents a commanding margin — nearly one-third more output than second-place Stanford — and reflects the breadth of social science research conducted across Harvard's schools and affiliated research centers, with IQSS at the center of that ecosystem.

## Harvard's Broader Research Leadership

The Nature Index ranking affirms Harvard's sustained commitment to advancing social science knowledge. Harvard also leads the Nature Index health-sciences ranking and ranks among the top academic institutions globally in biological sciences. IQSS plays a central role in this success, providing the infrastructure, data resources, methodological expertise, and collaborative environment that enable faculty, students, and staff across the University to produce research at the highest levels.
