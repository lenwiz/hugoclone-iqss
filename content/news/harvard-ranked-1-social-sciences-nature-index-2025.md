Harvard University has been ranked **#1 in the world** for social-sciences research in the inaugural Nature Index 2025 social-sciences ranking, published July 15, 2026. Harvard achieved an article Share of **68.61** — more than 16 points ahead of second-ranked Stanford University (52.04) — out of a field that includes institutions from across the globe.

The Nature Index, published by *Nature*, is one of the most authoritative measures of high-quality research output, tracking articles published in a curated set of leading peer-reviewed journals. Social sciences was added to the Index for the first time in June 2025, encompassing fields including economics, political science, sociology, anthropology, law, and education.

## IQSS Cited as a Key Factor

The Nature Index article specifically names the Institute for Quantitative Social Science as a facility that supports Harvard's world-leading social sciences output:

> *"Facilities that support Harvard's world-leading output in the social sciences include the Institute for Quantitative Social Science, which manages the Henry A. Murray Research Archive — a repository of more than 100 terabytes of data, audio and video collected from social-science research projects. Researchers from across the world can access the archive."*

The Henry A. Murray Research Archive, managed by IQSS, provides open access to longitudinal datasets, qualitative recordings, and research materials spanning decades of social science inquiry. Its scale and accessibility make it a unique resource for scholars worldwide and a significant contributor to the volume and quality of research Harvard produces.

Harvard's Share of 68.61 represents a dominant margin — nearly one-third more output than second-place Stanford — and reflects the breadth of social science research conducted across Harvard's schools and affiliated research centers, with IQSS at the center of that ecosystem.

## Harvard's Broader Research Leadership

The Nature Index ranking affirms Harvard's sustained commitment to advancing social science knowledge. Harvard also leads the Nature Index health-sciences ranking and ranks among the top academic institutions globally in biological sciences. IQSS plays a central role in this success, providing the infrastructure, data resources, methodological expertise, and collaborative environment that enable faculty and students across the University to produce research at the highest levels.
