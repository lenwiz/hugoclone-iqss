## Investigating Flight Attendants' Safety

<img src="../images/impact-flight-attendant.jpg" alt="Flight attendant walking down airplane aisle" style="width:200px;float:right;margin:0 0 15px 20px;border-radius:8px;" />

The longest-running longitudinal study of its kind, led by Dr. Eileen McNeely, executive director of [SHINE](https://shine.sph.harvard.edu/) at IQSS. The study collected data on uniforms from major US airlines. Laboratory tests found harmful substances on a manufacturer's uniforms. Analysis showed a connection between uniform introduction and reports of debilitating medical issues including respiratory problems and thyroid disease.

Findings provided evidence in a class action lawsuit holding the uniform manufacturer accountable.

[Read more about the SHINE study](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2023/11/harvard-study-plays-key-role-in-suit-against-airline-uniform-maker/)

## Digitizing History for the Future of Data Research

The first major endeavor to represent a significant Harvard Library collection as data. The IQSS Dataverse Project collaborated with the Houghton Library to create the Slavery, Abolition, Emancipation, and Freedom (SAEF) Data Collection — an open access research dataset of more than 1,200 items relating to African American history from the Early Republic through Reconstruction.

[Read the full story](../news/dataverse-publishes-houghton-library-saef-data-collection/)

## Measuring Population Well-Being with Social Media

The question of whether useful information can be derived from social media posts about movies, food, or seemingly nothing in particular. Devika Kakkar's team at the Center for Geographical Analysis created the Twitter Sentiment Geographical Index (TSGI), collaborating with other researchers to study topics such as pollution, climate change, and emotional well-being.

[Read the full story](../news/twitter-sentiment-geographical-index/)

IQSS Data Privacy Lab alumnus Jinyan Zang, founder and COO of Axle, has created a data platform to transform trucking logistics.

## Our Community

<img src="../images/impact-team.jpg" alt="IQSS team members" style="width:200px;float:right;margin:0 0 15px 20px;border-radius:8px;" />

IQSS is comprised of faculty and students representing nearly every school within Harvard, plus visiting scholars, fellows, and postdocs from institutions across the globe. These scholars are supported by a staff of more than 50 dedicated IT professionals, administrators, and support personnel.

Our philosophy: the more information we share, the better the social science research and the more likely it is that we will be able to fix some of the world's most complex social problems.
