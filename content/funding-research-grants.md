Harvard graduate students currently enrolled in a doctoral program and affiliated with IQSS can apply for funding to support dissertation research-related costs.

## Key Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | IQSS Harvard graduate student affiliates currently enrolled in a doctoral program, not graduating this year |
| **Amount** | $3,000 maximum |
| **Deadline** | Currently on hold |
| **Notification** | April; award distribution in early June |

## Eligible Expenses

- Survey development
- Crowdsourcing, human subjects and payments
- Supplies
- Technical services
- Printing and publication costs
- International travel
- Research assistance for specialized tasks

These funds may supplement other grant programs. Funds from this grant are not meant to be used for non-specialized research assistance or as a personal stipend.

## How to Apply

Submit a completed IQSS Graduate Student Funding Application via [CARAT](https://carat.fas.harvard.edu/applicant/newApplication?applicationPurposeId=4&fundingSourceId=7265).

### Required Materials:

- Application form
- Detailed budget
- Letter of recommendation from a faculty member

## Community Expectations

Grant recipients are expected to participate in the IQSS community. Recipients must also submit a one- to three-page narrative report describing their research, which may be featured on the IQSS website.
