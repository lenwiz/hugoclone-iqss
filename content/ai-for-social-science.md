## Overview

This page is a curated entry point — each section links to tools, workshops, and readings for deeper exploration.

## Research Methods

Brief overviews of the main AI/ML methods used in social science research, with key tools and Harvard context. Each section links to curated resources for deeper exploration.

### Large Language Models

<abbr title="Large Language Models">LLMs</abbr> like [Claude](https://www.huit.harvard.edu/anthropic-claude) (available via HUIT) and [GPT-4o](https://atg.fas.harvard.edu/ai-at-fas) can classify survey responses, code interview transcripts, screen literature, and generate synthetic respondents at scale. At IQSS, researchers use LLMs to code open-ended [CES](https://cces.gov.harvard.edu/) responses and summarize [Murray Archive](https://murray.harvard.edu/) transcripts.

**Key tools:** [Claude (HUIT)](https://www.huit.harvard.edu/anthropic-claude) · [GPT-4o (FAS AT)](https://atg.fas.harvard.edu/ai-at-fas) · [Llama 3 (open weights, FASRC)](https://www.rc.fas.harvard.edu/) · [Elicit](https://elicit.com/) · [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/) · [Semantic Scholar API](https://api.semanticscholar.org/)

**Prompt engineering essentials:** Provide a full codebook (not just a label); request JSON output with label, confidence, and rationale; validate against 100–200 hand-coded items (target Cohen's κ ≥ 0.80); set temperature = 0 for reproducibility; freeze the model version; publish prompts as supplementary materials in [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/).

**Featured paper:** Argyle et al. (2023), ["Out of One, Many"](https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2023.2) — *Political Analysis* — shows GPT-3 can simulate survey responses that closely match human opinion distributions across demographic groups.

### Natural Language Processing

Transformer-based <abbr title="Natural Language Processing">NLP</abbr> methods are the workhorse of computational social science. The [China Biographical Database (CBDB)](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/cbdb/home) at IQSS applies <abbr title="Named Entity Recognition">NER</abbr> and entity resolution across 2,000 years of Chinese biographical records — one of the largest historical NLP projects in the humanities.

**Core methods:** Sentiment analysis · Topic modeling · Named entity recognition · Stance and framing detection · Text scaling (Wordfish, Wordscores)

**Python:** [spaCy](https://spacy.io/) · [HuggingFace Transformers](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/) · [BERTopic](https://maartengr.github.io/BERTopic/) · [Top2Vec](https://github.com/ddangelov/Top2Vec) · [VADER](https://github.com/cjhutto/vaderSentiment) · [FinBERT](https://huggingface.co/ProsusAI/finbert)

**R:** [quanteda](https://quanteda.io/) · [tidytext](https://juliasilge.github.io/tidytext/) · [stm](https://www.structuraltopicmodel.com/) · [text](https://r-text.org/)

**Workshop:** [DSS text analysis materials](https://iqss.github.io/dss-workshops/) · [IQSS live schedule](https://www.iq.harvard.edu/training-workshops)

### Computer Vision

Computer vision enables extraction of information from images and video at scale. Harvard's [Center for Geographic Analysis (CGA)](https://gis.harvard.edu/) offers [2026 workshops](https://www.iq.harvard.edu/news/2025/12/explore-cgas-2026-training-schedule) on combining satellite imagery with GIS tools and LLMs.

**Core uses:** Satellite imagery (poverty, conflict, deforestation) · Historical photograph analysis · Social media image coding · Street-level imagery · Facial analysis *(high bias risk — audit before use)*

**Key tools:** [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) · [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/) · [CLIP](https://openai.com/research/clip) · [Roboflow](https://roboflow.com/) · [Google Vision API](https://cloud.google.com/vision) · [Sentinel-2 (ESA)](https://sentinel.esa.int/web/sentinel/missions/sentinel-2)

**Featured paper:** Jean et al. (2016), ["Combining satellite imagery and machine learning to predict poverty"](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf7894) — *Science* — uses CNN transfer learning on daytime satellite imagery to predict household wealth at village level in Africa.

### Causal Inference with Machine Learning

Causal ML combines credible causal identification with ML's predictive power. [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/) hosts thousands of published replication datasets — a useful benchmark for validating implementations.

**Core methods:** [Double/Debiased ML](https://docs.doubleml.org/) · [Causal Forests (grf)](https://grf-labs.github.io/grf/) · [BART (bartCause)](https://cran.r-project.org/package=bartCause) · [Synthetic Control (augsynth)](https://github.com/ebenmichael/augsynth) · [Staggered DiD (did)](https://bcallaway11.github.io/did/)

**Python:** [EconML (Microsoft)](https://github.com/py-why/EconML) · [DoubleML](https://docs.doubleml.org/)

**Key readings:** [Cunningham, *The Mixtape*](https://mixtape.scunning.com/) (free) · [Huntington-Klein, *The Effect*](https://theeffectbook.net/) (free) · Athey & Imbens (2019), *Annual Review of Economics*

### Survey Research and Measurement

AI extends the reach of survey methods — automating open-end coding, linking records across datasets, and harmonizing questions across instruments.

**Core uses:** Automated open-end coding · Adaptive survey design · Record linkage and deduplication · Cross-instrument question harmonization

**Key tools:** [fastLink (R)](https://github.com/kosukeimai/fastLink) · [dedupe (Python)](https://github.com/dedupeio/dedupe) · [sentence-transformers](https://www.sbert.net/) · [CES public data](https://cces.gov.harvard.edu/)

**IQSS resource:** The [CES open-ended response archive](https://cces.gov.harvard.edu/) is a large public benchmark for coding validation. Contact [DSS](https://dss.iq.harvard.edu) for guidance on validation design.

### Network Analysis

Network methods identify community structure, track temporal evolution, and integrate text-derived ties with relational data. Applicable to citation networks, legislative cosponsorship, social media graphs, and co-authorship data.

**Key tools:** [NetworkX (Python)](https://networkx.org/) · [igraph (R/Python)](https://igraph.org/) · [text2map (R)](https://github.com/culturalcartography/text2map) · [HDBSCAN](https://hdbscan.readthedocs.io/)

## Harvard Research Highlights

Selected studies illustrating AI methods in social science, with Harvard and IQSS connections:

**King, Pan & Roberts (2013)** — [How Censorship in China Allows Government Criticism but Silences Collective Expression](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055413000014) — *American Political Science Review* — [Gary King](https://gking.harvard.edu/) (IQSS Director) and collaborators analyzed millions of Chinese social media posts using NLP to reveal that censorship targets collective action, not criticism. A landmark in computational political science. [Replication data](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/) deposited at Harvard Dataverse.

**Argyle et al. (2023)** — [Out of One, Many: Using Language Models to Simulate Human Samples](https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2023.2) — *Political Analysis* — Demonstrates that GPT-3 can replicate U.S. public opinion distributions across demographic groups, opening new possibilities — and cautions — for synthetic data in survey research.

**Salganik et al. (2020)** — [Measuring the Predictability of Life Outcomes with a Scientific Mass Collaboration](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1915006117) — *PNAS* — The Fragile Families Challenge recruited 160 teams to predict six life outcomes using ML. Finding: even the best models had modest predictive power, highlighting the gap between prediction and causal understanding. A benchmark result for CSS researchers.

**Jean et al. (2016)** — [Combining Satellite Imagery and Machine Learning to Predict Poverty](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf7894) — *Science* — Transfer learning on daytime satellite imagery predicts household wealth with accuracy comparable to survey methods, at a fraction of the cost. Widely extended at Harvard's [Center for Geographic Analysis](https://gis.harvard.edu/).

**Buolamwini & Gebru (2018)** — [Gender Shades: Intersectional Accuracy Disparities in Commercial Gender Classification](http://proceedings.mlr.press/v81/buolamwini18a.html) — *FAccT* — Audits three commercial facial analysis systems, finding error rates up to 34.7 percentage points higher for darker-skinned women than lighter-skinned men. Required reading before any facial CV use in research.

**Grimmer, Roberts & Stewart (2022)** — [Text as Data: A New Framework for Machine Learning and the Social Sciences](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691207551/text-as-data) — Princeton University Press — The definitive methodological guide to computational text analysis: what the methods can and cannot do, and how to validate them. Margaret Roberts was an IQSS postdoctoral fellow.

## Research Ethics and Data Policy

### Data Privacy and Classification

Before using any AI tool with research data, verify its [Harvard classification level](https://policy.security.harvard.edu/data-classification). HUIT AI tools (Claude, Gemini, GPT-4o) are approved for **DSL1–3** only. Data at **DSL4 or above** — HIPAA-covered records, SSNs, FERPA-protected student data — must not be sent to commercial APIs. For sensitive data, run open-weight models ([Llama 3](https://www.rc.fas.harvard.edu/), [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/)) on FASRC, where data stays within Harvard's infrastructure. De-identify before processing even DSL2/3 text corpora.

### Validity and Bias

Audit for differential performance across demographic subgroups before relying on AI labels in any analysis involving race, gender, or national origin. LLMs encode training-data biases; classifications of politically sensitive content require human validation. Report inter-rater reliability (Cohen's κ or Krippendorff's α) between AI and human coders in your methods section.

### Reproducibility

Report model name, version, and exact prompt for all AI-generated labels — these are methodological choices that affect replication. Freeze model versions (e.g., `claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022`) to prevent silent API updates from affecting results. Deposit prompts and validation codebooks in [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/).

### IRB Considerations

Analyzing existing human-generated text with LLMs generally does not require new IRB approval if the original collection was approved. Publishing synthetic respondent data as if it were real survey data requires IRB consultation. Automated systems affecting research subjects directly (adaptive interventions, content moderation) typically require explicit IRB review.

## Getting Started at IQSS

<div class="info-box" role="note" aria-label="IQSS Data Science Services consultation information">
<p><strong>Data Science Services (<abbr title="Data Science Services">DSS</abbr>)</strong> offers individual consultations on research design, statistical methods, and AI/ML for <abbr title="Institute for Quantitative Social Science">IQSS</abbr> affiliates. Schedule at <a href="https://dss.iq.harvard.edu">dss.iq.harvard.edu</a> or email <a href="mailto:help@iq.harvard.edu">help@iq.harvard.edu</a>.</p>
</div>

**New to AI/ML in research:** Start with an [IQSS DSS workshop](https://www.iq.harvard.edu/training-workshops), work through [self-paced DSS materials](https://iqss.github.io/dss-workshops/) on R or Python, and read Salganik's [*Bit by Bit*](https://www.bitbybitbook.com) (free online) for the research design foundations. The [Harvard Library AI Guide](https://guides.library.harvard.edu/airesearch) offers curated tool comparisons by discipline.

**Have programming experience:** Access [HUIT's Claude](https://www.huit.harvard.edu/anthropic-claude) and run a pilot coding task on a small hand-labeled sample. Work through the [HuggingFace NLP course](https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/) (free), explore [tidymodels](https://www.tidymodels.org/) (R) or [scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/) (Python), and book a [DSS consultation](https://dss.iq.harvard.edu) for validation design before scaling.

**Ready to scale:** Request [FASRC access](https://www.rc.fas.harvard.edu/) for GPU-enabled jobs. Run sensitive or DSL4+ data locally on open-weight models. Deposit data, code, and prompts in [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/). Contact [DSS](https://dss.iq.harvard.edu) for a consultation on your specific research design.

## IQSS AI Initiatives

IQSS is actively building infrastructure and programming to support AI-enabled social science research across Harvard:

- **[AI Literacy for Researchers](https://www.iq.harvard.edu/training-workshops)** — Workshop series on responsible AI use in research design, data collection, and analysis.
- **Computational Social Science Working Group** — Monthly meetings for researchers using computational methods; presentations and code-sharing welcome. Contact [info@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:info@iq.harvard.edu).
- **AI & Research Ethics Forum** — Joint initiative with the Harvard Data Science Initiative on governance, bias, and transparency in AI-assisted research.
- **[OpenDP](https://opendp.org/)** — IQSS-developed differential privacy toolkit, adopted by the U.S. Census Bureau and other statistical agencies.
- **[Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/)** — IQSS-maintained open data repository (100,000+ datasets) with AI-assisted discovery via integrated AI Guide and MCP server (v6.7+).
- **[Cooperative Election Study (CES)](https://cces.gov.harvard.edu/)** — Harvard's flagship 60,000-respondent election survey, widely used as an AI coding benchmark.
- **[China Biographical Database (CBDB)](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/cbdb/home)** — IQSS historical database applying NER and entity resolution across 2,000 years of Chinese biographical records.
- **[Murray Research Archive](https://murray.harvard.edu/)** — Harvard's longitudinal human subjects data archive, supporting text analysis workflows for researchers.
- **[Center for Geographic Analysis (CGA)](https://gis.harvard.edu/)** — IQSS-affiliated center running [2026 GIS and AI workshops](https://www.iq.harvard.edu/news/2025/12/explore-cgas-2026-training-schedule) including LLM-based geospatial classification.
- **GPU Cluster Access** — IQSS researchers can request priority [FASRC](https://www.rc.fas.harvard.edu/) access for training and fine-tuning models on large research corpora.

For more information, contact [IQSS Research Computing](mailto:info@iq.harvard.edu).
