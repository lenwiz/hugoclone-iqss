Affiliates seeking to connect with researchers, colleagues, or simply needing workspace are welcome to use the IQSS office space.

Affiliates can make temporary desk reservations through the Robin booking platform.

<a href="https://dashboard.robinpowered.com/iqss/login" class="cta-button" target="_blank">Book a Desk via Robin</a>

## Logging in to Robin

Users should log in with their **g.harvard.edu email address**. Non-Harvard affiliates must email robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu to be manually added.

1. Navigate to the [IQSS Robin login page](https://dashboard.robinpowered.com/iqss/login)
2. Click "Continue with Google" and authenticate with g.harvard.edu credentials

## Making a Reservation

After account setup, you can book desks through the Robin platform.

**Key rules:**
- Please only reserve desks for the time you will be using it
- Users can book up to 90 days in advance

## Room Access & Security

When reserving a desk, email **robinhelp@iq.harvard.edu** at least one business day in advance so a key can be prepared. At the end of your final booking day, return keys to the mailbox of the IQSS Office Manager, Emma Loayza.

## Reserve via Mobile App

In addition to web browser booking, you can download the Robin mobile app:

- [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/robin-powered/id948856282)
- [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.robinpowered.compass)
