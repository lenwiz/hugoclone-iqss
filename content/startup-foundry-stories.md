## TeamBirth / Unravel Healthcare

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<img src="https://www.iq.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8171/files/styles/hwp_1_1__360x360_scale/public/harvard-iqss/files/unravel-healthcare-collage.jpg" alt="Unravel Healthcare" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;" />
<h3><a href="https://www.unravelhealthcare.com/" target="_blank">Unravel Healthcare</a></h3>
<p>Born from Ariadne Labs research, TeamBirth redesigned childbirth care processes to improve communication and teamwork. After scaling to 200+ hospitals, it spun out as Unravel Healthcare, led by Dr. Amber Weiseth. The Startup Foundry provided mentorship, product design workshops, data science support, an HBS MBA intern, and matched them with a COO.</p>

## MorphoAI

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<img src="https://www.iq.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8171/files/styles/hwp_1_1__360x360_scale/public/harvard-iqss/files/morphoai-logo.png" alt="MorphoAI" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;" />
<h3><a href="https://www.morpho.ai/" target="_blank">MorphoAI</a></h3>
<p>Founded by Dr. Andy Spielberg from his postdoctoral work at Harvard's Lewis Lab, MorphoAI uses AI-driven digital twins to accelerate design of robots, medical devices, and industrial machinery. Received a £2.5M UK government (ARIA) grant in January 2025 and was accepted into Y Combinator in May 2025.</p>

## terraFlow

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<img src="https://www.iq.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8171/files/styles/hwp_1_1__360x360_scale/public/2025-09/terraflow-logo-black_bkgd.png" alt="terraFlow" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;" />
<h3><a href="http://terraflow.app/" target="_blank">terraFlow</a></h3>
<p>Founded by Harvard PhD student Dan Freeman, terraFlow uses AI to accelerate drug discovery by combining pharma internal data with scientific literature to uncover biomarkers. Closed a $750K pre-seed round (including $600K from Underscore VC), piloted with AstraZeneca, Vertex, Regeneron, Boehringer Ingelheim, and Eli Lilly, and built a $600K+ sales pipeline.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Memorious

<div class="card">

<h3><a href="http://memorious.io" target="_blank">Memorious</a></h3>
<p>Co-founded by Spandan Madan (Harvard CS PhD) and Gabriel Kreiman (Harvard Professor of Neuroscience and AI), Memorious aims to give humans infinite memory using AI. Creates a "Memorome" for each user from conversations, documents, photos, and location data. Closed a $3M pre-seed round led by Mayfield Fund.</p>

## Thresher (now Two Six Technologies M3)

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<img src="https://www.iq.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8171/files/styles/hwp_1_1__360x360_scale/public/2025-10/Two-Six-Tech-Products-M3-Logo-and-Text%20sq2.jpg" alt="Thresher / M3" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;" />
<h3>Thresher / Two Six Technologies M3</h3>
<p>Co-founded in 2015 by Professor Gary King from Harvard IQSS research on Chinese social media censorship and automated text analysis. Bootstrapped and profitable from day one. Acquired in May 2022 by Two Six Technologies (backed by Carlyle Group) and rebranded as the Media Manipulation Monitor (M3) for national security intelligence.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Neru Health

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="card">
<img src="https://www.iq.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8171/files/styles/hwp_1_1__360x360_scale/public/2025-10/neru-logo.png" alt="Neru Health" style="width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:4px;" />
<h3>Neru Health</h3>
<p>Founded by Morgan Moncada (Harvard MBA, Blavatnik Healthcare Fellow), Catherine Tadina, and Maria Grebenshchikova. Pivoted from a consumer sleep app to a B2B vertical AI platform automating patient support for DME providers. Raised $4M at a $16M valuation cap. Shows 10–20% adherence improvement and up to 60% labor cost reduction.</p>
</div>
