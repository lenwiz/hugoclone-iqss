The annual Eric M. Mindich Encounters with Authors Conference is an opportunity for one or more authors to present a major work still in progress.

## Key Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | IQSS faculty affiliates |
| **Amount** | $10,000 maximum |
| **Deadline** | Rolling |

IQSS covers all conference expenses, arranges invitations, and provides necessary administrative and staff support.

## How to Apply

Contact [funding@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:funding@iq.harvard.edu) to submit a proposal, including:

- Potential speakers
- Idea for conference topic
- Proposed date range
