## Arthur Kleinman

**Esther and Sidney Rabb Professor of Anthropology**

Arthur Kleinman is a psychiatrist and anthropologist who has spent over four decades teaching at Harvard Medical School's Department of Global Health and Social Medicine and in the FAS Department of Anthropology. He chaired the former department from 1989-2000 and the latter from 2004-2007, and held the Presley Professorship at HMS from 1993-2000. From 2008 to 2016, he served as the Victor and William Fung Faculty Director of Harvard's Asia Center.

Kleinman has authored 7 books as sole author, including *The Illness Narratives*, *Rethinking Psychiatry*, *What Really Matters*, and *The Soul of Care*. He co-authored 4 additional books and co-edited 29 volumes, among them *Social Suffering*, *Culture and Depression*, and *World Mental Health*. He has produced over 350 journal articles and book chapters and served as founding Editor-in-Chief of *Culture, Medicine and Psychiatry* from 1976-1986.

His current work includes a book on caregiving drawing on his articles in the *Lancet* and *New England Journal of Medicine*. His ongoing projects involve a comparative study of eldercare for dementia across six Asian settings, an ethnographic study examining trust in doctor-patient relationships in China, planning for a major meeting on global mental health services delivery, and development of Southeast Asia studies at Harvard.
