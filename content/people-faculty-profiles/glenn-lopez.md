## Glenn Lopez

**Director of Engineering, VPAL**

Glenn is the Director of Engineering for VPAL (Vice Provost for Advances in Learning) at Harvard. Since 2014, he has developed multi-cloud platform software to address the challenge of connecting teaching and learning data from decentralized systems across Harvard. These efforts matured into a cloud data platform infrastructure powering H.U.D.A.T. (Harvard University Data Aggregation and Transformation System), which is described as "a comprehensive centralized database that links critical teaching and learning management systems (LMS) at Harvard." This work has enabled various projects including Harvard Link, Syllabus Explorer, platform data analytics, and research.

As the technical lead for a team of data scientists and engineers, Glenn focuses on advancing the architectural vision for the VPAL cloud data platform and expanding its capabilities for future teaching and learning experiences.

Glenn has 15 years of software engineering and systems design experience. He received an M.Eng. in Systems Engineering from Cornell University and a B.S. in Computer Engineering from UC Irvine. Prior to Harvard, he "worked as a Principal Systems Engineer for 9 years" developing embedded software, architecting avionics data bus software interfaces, and implementing avionics data analytics visualization and algorithm simulation software.

His interests outside of work include cooking, playing video games with his son, and Texas hold 'em poker with friends.
