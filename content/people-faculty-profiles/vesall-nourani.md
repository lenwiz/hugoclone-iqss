## Vesall Nourani

**Assistant Professor, Harvard Graduate School of Education**

Vesall Nourani is an assistant professor at HGSE. His research explores "the roles of knowledge and learning in economic activity across sub-Saharan Africa." He conducts research on educational programs promoting community-based research, investigation, and dialogue aimed at advancing community development. His current projects use randomized controlled trials and other causal inference tools to study program impacts in Uganda and Colombia.
