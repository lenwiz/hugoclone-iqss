## Kenneth Shepsle

**George D. Markham Professor of Government**

Kenneth A. Shepsle is the George D. Markham Professor of Government. He has authored or co-authored numerous books including *Politics in Plural Societies: A Theory of Democratic Instability*, *The Giant Jigsaw Puzzle: Democratic Committee Assignments in the Modern House*, *Models of Multiparty Electoral Competition*, *Making and Breaking Governments*, *American Government: Power and Purpose*, and *Analyzing Politics*. He has edited or co-edited works such as *The Congressional Budget Process: Some Views From the Inside*, *Political Equilibrium*, *Perspectives on Positive Political Economy*, and *Cabinet Ministers and Parliamentary Government*.

He has written extensively on formal political theory, congressional politics, public policy, and political economy. His fellowships include a National Fellowship at the Hoover Institution, a Fellowship at the Center for Advanced Study in the Behavioral Sciences, and a Guggenheim Fellowship. He served as editor of "Public Choice" from 1975 to 1980. In 1990 he was elected to both the National Academy of Sciences and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. His current research focuses on "formal models of political institutions, especially as it applied to the interface between politics, intergenerational political economy, and demography."
