## Hillel Bavli

**Assistant Professor of Law, SMU Dedman School of Law**

Hillel Bavli is an Assistant Professor of Law at SMU Dedman School of Law and an affiliate of the Institute for Quantitative Social Science. He is described as "an experienced litigation attorney" with teaching and scholarship interests in "evidence law, tort law, complex litigation, law and economics, and empirical legal studies." He is noted as being "particularly interested in applications of statistics to law."

Regarding his academic background, he "completed a Fulbright Fellowship studying game theory in Jerusalem, Israel" and "holds an A.M. in Statistics and a Ph.D. in Statistics in Law and Government, in addition to a J.D."
