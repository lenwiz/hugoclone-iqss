## David Cutler

**Otto Eckstein Professor of Applied Economics**

David Cutler has built a notable career spanning academia and public service. He "served as Assistant Professor of Economics from 1991 to 1995" and "was named John L. Loeb Associate Professor of Social Sciences in 1995," receiving tenure in 1997. He currently holds the position of Otto Eckstein Professor of Applied Economics in the Department of Economics and Kennedy School of Government, and he completed a five-year term as associate dean of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences for Social Sciences.

Recognized for both scholarly contributions and mentorship of graduate students, his work in health economics and public economics has brought him considerable recognition. He "served on the Council of Economic Advisers and the National Economic Council during the Clinton Administration" and advised the presidential campaigns of Bill Bradley, John Kerry, and Barack Obama. He has also held positions with the National Institutes of Health and the National Academy of Sciences, and is a Research Associate at the National Bureau of Economic Research and a member of the Institute of Medicine.

Cutler authored *Your Money Or Your Life: Strong Medicine for America's Health Care System*, published by Oxford University Press. His ideas were featured in a *New York Times* Magazine article called *The Quality Cure* by Roger Lowenstein. He was named one of the 30 people who could powerfully impact healthcare by *Modern Healthcare* magazine and one of the 50 most influential men aged 45 and younger by *Details* magazine.
