## Brian Schaffner

**Professor of Political Science, Tufts University**

Brian Schaffner is the Newhouse Professor of Civic Studies in Political Science and Tisch College at Tufts University. His research focuses on "public opinion, campaigns and elections, political parties, and race and prejudice in American politics." He co-authored *Hometown Inequality: Race, Class, and Representation in American Local Politics* and *Campaign Finance and Political Polarization: When Purists Prevail*.

He serves as a Principal Investigator for the Cooperative Election Study, reviewing editor of PNAS Nexus, and sits on the advisory panel of the British Election Study. In 2024, he received an Andrew Carnegie fellowship to support a co-authored book project titled *American Mosaic*.
