## Eric Mazur

**Balkanski Professor of Physics and Applied Physics**

Eric Mazur is the Balkanski Professor of Physics and Applied Physics and Area Chair of Applied Physics at Harvard University, Member of the Faculty of Education at the Harvard Graduate School of Education, and Past President of the Optical Society.

He is described as "a prominent physicist known for his contributions in nanophotonics, an internationally recognized educational innovator," and a widely sought speaker. In education, he is best known for Peer Instruction, an interactive teaching method designed to engage students both in and outside the classroom. In 2014, he became the first recipient of the Minerva Prize for Advancements in Higher Education. He has received numerous awards in both physics and education and has founded several successful companies.

Mazur has published extensively in peer-reviewed journals and holds numerous patents. He authored *Peer Instruction: A User's Manual* (Prentice Hall, 1997), which explains interactive teaching in large lectures, and *Principles and Practice of Physics* (Pearson, 2015), which introduces a new approach to teaching introductory calculus-based physics.

He is a leading speaker on optics and education, and his "motivational lectures on interactive teaching, educational technology, and assessment have inspired people around the world" to transform their teaching approaches.
