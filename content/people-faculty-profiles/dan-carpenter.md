## Dan Carpenter

**Allie S. Freed Professor of Government & Chair of the Department of Government, Harvard University**

Daniel Carpenter's research spans political institutions, North American political history, political economy of government regulation, bureaucratic politics and organization, stochastic models of behavioral and bounded rationality, and media behavior and agenda setting.

His major ongoing project is the FDA Project — a large-scale theoretical, historical and statistical analysis of pharmaceutical regulation in the United States.

### Recent Work

- Published on public trust in the FDA via a national survey study (2024, *Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics*)
- Explored the concept of "An FDA for AI?" examining approval regulation for frontier AI models (2024, AAAI/ACM Conference)
- Co-authored work on congressional petitioning from 1789-1950 with Maggie Blackhawk (Oxford University Press, 2024)
- Working paper on "Heuristic Agenda Closure in Administrative Government"
- Delivered the John Gaus Award Lecture 2023, titled "Toward a Theory of Office"
- Published "Empire, Ministry, Indigeneity" in *Clio* (2024)
