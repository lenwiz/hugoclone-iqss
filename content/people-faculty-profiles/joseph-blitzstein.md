## Joseph Blitzstein

**Professor of the Practice in Statistics**

**Email:** [blitz@fas.harvard.edu](mailto:blitz@fas.harvard.edu)

Joe Blitzstein is a Professor of the Practice in Statistics at Harvard University. He completed his Ph.D. at Stanford University in 2006, with advisor Persi Diaconis. His research combines "statistics, probability, and combinatorics." He is particularly interested in "graphical models, complex networks, and Monte Carlo algorithms" including "sequential importance sampling and Markov chains."
