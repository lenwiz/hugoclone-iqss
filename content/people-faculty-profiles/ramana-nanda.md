## Ramana Nanda

**Sarofim-Rock Professor of Business Administration**

Ramana Nanda is the Sarofim-Rock Professor of Business Administration and Co-Director of the Private Capital Project at Harvard Business School. His research focuses on "financing frictions facing new ventures," aiming to help entrepreneurs with fundraising and to illuminate how "financial intermediaries, corporate R&D and policy makers can improve the odds of selecting and commercializing the most promising ideas and technologies in the economy."
