## Fiery Cushman

**John L. Loeb Associate Professor of the Social Sciences**

Cushman holds the title of John L. Loeb Associate Professor of the Social Sciences, FAS.

His research focuses on how people learn and make decisions in social settings, with a particular emphasis on moral judgment and decision-making. His current research projects explore questions such as: "Why, and how, did punishment evolve?" and "What are the emotional systems that prevent us from doing harm to each other?" He also investigates how humans interpret each other's behavior and how people decide which actions are worth considering among all possibilities. He combines "empirical methods with computational modeling in order to make progress on these questions."
