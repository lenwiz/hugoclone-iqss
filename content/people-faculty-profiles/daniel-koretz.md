## Daniel Koretz

**Research Professor on Education**

Daniel Koretz is an expert on educational assessment and testing policy. His work has primarily focused on the impact of high-stakes testing. His research has included studies of score inflation, effects of testing programs on educational practice, assessment of students with disabilities, international differences in the variability of student achievement, application of value-added models to educational achievement, and development of methods for validating scores under high-stakes conditions. His current work focuses on "the equity implications of high-stakes testing, the effects of high-stakes testing on postsecondary outcomes," characteristics of traditional tests that encourage inappropriate test preparation, and the design and evaluation of new testing designs tailored for accountability. He is a member of the National Academy of Education and a Fellow of the American Educational Research Association. His doctorate is in developmental psychology from Cornell University. Before obtaining his degree, he "taught emotionally disturbed students in public elementary and junior high schools."
