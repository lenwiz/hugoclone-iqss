## Peter Bol

**Charles H. Carswell Professor of East Asian Languages and Civilizations, Harvard University**

Peter Bol is a scholar of Chinese intellectual and cultural history. His research focuses on the history of China's cultural elites, both nationally and locally, spanning the 7th to 17th centuries. He also works extensively in geospatial analysis and digital humanities projects related to Chinese history.

He authored *"This Culture of Ours": Intellectual Transitions in T'ang and Sung China*, *Neo-Confucianism in History*, and *Localizing Learning: The Literati Enterprise in Wuzhou, 1100-1600*.

He founded and directed Harvard's Center for Geographic Analysis (2005), advancing geospatial analysis across the university. As Vice Provost (2013-2018), he oversaw HarvardX, the Harvard Initiative in Learning and Teaching, and research linking online and residential learning.

He directs two major collaborative digital projects:

- **China Historical Geographic Information Systems (CHGIS)** — a Harvard-Fudan University collaboration building a GIS for 2,000 years of Chinese history
- **China Biographical Database (CBDB)** — a Harvard, Academia Sinica, and Peking University project, an online relational database currently of over 640,000 historical figures

He co-teaches ChinaX and the Return of China as HarvardX online courses with William Kirby.
