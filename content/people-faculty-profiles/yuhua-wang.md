## Yuhua Wang

**Associate Professor of Government**

He is an Associate Professor in the Department of Government at Harvard University whose research focuses on "state institutions and state-business relations in China." He authored *Tying the Autocrat's Hands: The Rise of the Rule of Law in China* (Cambridge University Press, 2015).

He earned his B.A. and M.A. from Peking University in 2003 and 2006, and his Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Michigan in 2011. From 2011 to 2015, he served as assistant professor of political science at the University of Pennsylvania.
