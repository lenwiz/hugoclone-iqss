## Gautam Nair

**Assistant Professor of Public Policy, HKS**

Gautam Nair is an Assistant Professor of Public Policy at Harvard Kennedy School. He "conducts research in comparative politics and international political economy, and focus primarily on the politics of inequality, redistribution, and special interests in South Asia."
