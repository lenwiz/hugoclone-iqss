## Matthew Blackwell

**Associate Professor of Government, FAS**

**Email:** [mblackwell@gov.harvard.edu](mailto:mblackwell@gov.harvard.edu)

Matthew Blackwell is an Associate Professor of Government at Harvard University. He studies political methodology, focusing on "dynamic causal inference, instrumental variables, experimental design, missing data, and panel data." His substantive interests include "the lasting effects of slavery on political attitudes in the US South."
