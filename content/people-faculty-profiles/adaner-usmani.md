## Adaner Usmani

**Assistant Professor of Sociology**

He is an Assistant Professor of Sociology and Social Studies at Harvard University, having earned his Ph.D. in Sociology from New York University in May 2017. From 2017 to 2019, he served as a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Watson Institute at Brown University.

His research centers on questions of inequality — why some people flourish while others suffer, and what explains why "movements or policies emerge to challenge or moderate this inequality" in certain places.

In one area of his work, he explores when ordinary individuals manage to coordinate collective action and how these patterns explain the advancement of democracy. He also studies the origins and consequences of American mass incarceration.

He primarily uses "computational and quantitative methods," which he has taught at the graduate level, while maintaining "a keen interest in the comparative historical method, and in qualitative social science more generally."
