## Sebastian Munoz-Najar Galvez

**Bluhm Family Assistant Professor of Data Science and Education**

Sebastian Munoz-Najar Galvez is the Bluhm Family Assistant Professor of Data Science and Education at the Harvard Graduate School of Education. He earned his doctorate in Sociology of Education from Stanford University's Graduate School of Education. As a sociologist of science, he employs computational linguistics and network analysis to examine how research agendas evolve over time. More recently, he has shifted his focus toward "the study of adolescent experience, college admissions, and technologies of belonging." His research has appeared in the American Sociological Review, the Proceedings of the National Academy of Science, and American Educational Research Journal.
