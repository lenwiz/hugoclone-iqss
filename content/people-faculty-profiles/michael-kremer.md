## Michael Kremer

**Gates Professor of Developing Societies**

Michael Kremer is the Gates Professor of Developing Societies in the Department of Economics at Harvard University and Senior Fellow at the Brookings Institution. He is a Fellow of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, a recipient of a MacArthur Fellowship and a Presidential Faculty Fellowship, and was named a Young Global Leader by the World Economic Forum. His recent research focuses on "education and health in developing countries, immigration, and globalization." He and Rachel Glennerster co-authored *Strong Medicine: Creating Incentives for Pharmaceutical Research on Neglected Diseases*.
