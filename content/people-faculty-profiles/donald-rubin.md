## Donald Rubin

**John L. Loeb Professor of Statistics, Emeritus**

Professor Rubin's research interests include "causal inference in experiments and observational studies, inference in sample surveys with nonresponse and in missing data problems," application of Bayesian and empirical Bayesian techniques, and developing and applying statistical models to data across various scientific disciplines.

He holds the title of John L. Loeb Professor of Statistics, Emeritus, in the Department of Statistics at Harvard University. He is affiliated with the Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS) as Affiliated Faculty.
