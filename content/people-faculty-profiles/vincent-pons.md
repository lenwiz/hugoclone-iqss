## Vincent Pons

**Associate Professor, Harvard Business School**

He is "an Associate Professor at Harvard Business School" and affiliated with NBER and J-PAL.

His research focuses on understanding why representative democracies sometimes fail to produce leaders and policies that match citizens' preferences.

His work has been published in top journals including Econometrica, the American Economic Review, the Quarterly Journal of Economics, and the American Political Science Review, with media coverage in outlets like The New York Times and The Economist.

He holds a PhD in Economics from MIT, master's degrees in Economics from the Ecole Normale Supérieure in Paris (jointly with the Paris School of Economics and ENSAE), and in Political Philosophy from Université Paris 1, Panthéon-Sorbonne.
