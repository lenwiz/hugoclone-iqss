## Xiang Zhou

**Associate Professor of Sociology**

Xiang Zhou is an Associate Professor of Sociology at Harvard University. His research focuses on "quantitative methodology, causes and consequences of economic inequality, and political culture in contemporary China." He has published in several journals including *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, *Social Forces*, *Sociological Methodology*, *Sociological Research and Methods*, and *China Economic Review*. Prior to Harvard, he "worked as a postdoctoral research associate at Princeton University." He earned his PhD in Sociology and Statistics from the University of Michigan in 2015.
