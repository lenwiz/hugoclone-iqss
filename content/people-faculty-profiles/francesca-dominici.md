## Francesca Dominici

**Clarence James Gamble Professor of Biostatistics**

Dr. Dominici earned her "B.S. in Statistics from University La Sapienza in Rome, Italy" and her "Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Padua in Italy." She completed postdoctoral training with Scott L. Zeger and Jonathan M. Samet at Johns Hopkins University's Bloomberg School of Public Health. In 1999, she became Assistant Professor at the Bloomberg School and received promotion to Full Professor with tenure in 2007. She was recruited to Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health as a tenured Professor of Biostatistics in 2009, became Associate Dean of Information Technology in 2011, and "Senior Associate Dean for Research in 2013."

She holds the title of Clarence James Gamble Professor of Biostatistics, Population, and Data Science at the TH Chan School of Public Health, and serves as Faculty Co-Director of the Harvard Data Science Initiative.
