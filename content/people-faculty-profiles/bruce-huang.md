## Bruce Huang

**Data Science Lecturer, Harvard Extension School**

Dr. Bruce Huang serves as director of the master's degree program in information technology at Harvard Extension School, overseeing five ALM degree programs including computer science, cybersecurity, data science, digital media design, and systems engineering.

He is described as "a scholar-practitioner in the field of computer science." As a practitioner, he developed software products at IBM and his tech startups focused on enabling enterprises to test and manage performance of mission-critical infrastructure. He served as "a managing partner and practice executive at IBM Global Services" and was a VC-backed tech and education entrepreneur. He "was named to the 2020 MIT Alumni Association Honor Roll of Service."

His academic appointments have included positions at UCLA's School of Engineering, UCLA's Computer Science Department, UCLA International Institute Global Classroom, UCLA Extension (as Academic Department Director), Cornell University's Cornell Tech campus (as Visiting Lecturer), and the University of Maryland (as Adjunct Associate Professor).

At Maryland, he taught computer networking and communication protocol courses. At UCLA, he taught graduate and undergraduate classes in engineering entrepreneurship, data science, leadership and innovation, and computer science. He also created UCLA Extension's data science certificate program. At Cornell, he taught modern software development to graduate CS, MBA, and LLM students.

At Harvard, he "teaches the foundation of data science and engineering, data science and AI ethics, regulations, and laws" and guides ALM data science degree candidates on capstone projects.

His research interests span "nontraditional adult learner retention, education policy, data science and artificial intelligence ethics, performance measurements, and software testing."
