## Elizabeth Linos

**Associate Professor of Public Policy, HKS**

Dr. Elizabeth Linos is the Emma Bloomberg Associate Professor for Public Policy and Management. Her research primarily centers on improving government through its workforce and service delivery. She applies behavioral science insights and public management evidence to address three key areas: recruiting, retaining, and supporting government workers; reducing administrative burdens faced by low-income households in government interactions; and integrating evidence-based policymaking into government operations.

Her work has appeared in numerous academic journals including the Journal for Public Administration Research and Theory, Public Administration Review, Econometrica, American Economic Journal: Economic Policy, and Behavioural Public Policy. Media coverage of her research has appeared in the New York Times, The Economist, the BBC, NPR, Slate, and the Harvard Business Review.

Before her academic role, she served as VP and Head of Research and Evaluation at the Behavioral Insights Team in North America, where she collaborated with US and UK government agencies to improve programs through behavioral science and build rigorous evaluation capacity. Prior to that position, she worked directly in government as a policy advisor to Greek Prime Minister George Papandreou, with a focus on social innovation and public sector reform.
