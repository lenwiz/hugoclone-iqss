## Elhanan Helpman

**Galen L. Stone Professor of International Trade**

Elhanan Helpman holds the Galen L. Stone Professorship of International Trade at Harvard University and is a Fellow of the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research. He earned his B.A. in Economics and Statistics and M.A. in Economics from Tel Aviv University, and his Ph.D. in Economics from Harvard.

His contributions span balance of payments, exchange rate regimes, stabilization programs, and foreign debt. His most significant work covers international trade, economic growth, and political economy. He is "a cofounder of the 'new trade theory' and the 'new growth theory,'" which highlight economies of scale and imperfect competition. His work is summarized in seven books, co-authored with Paul Krugman and Gene Grossman, as well as solo volumes including *The Mystery of Economic Growth* and *Understanding Global Trade*.

He has studied the Israeli economy and participated in Israeli policy debates, serving on the Advisory Board of the Bank of Israel, the Council for National Planning, the National Council for Research and Development, and the board of Bank Hapoalim.

He co-edited the *Journal of International Economics* and the *Quarterly Journal of Economics* and edited the *European Economic Review*. He is a Fellow of the Econometric Society and served on its Council. He delivered major lectures including the Frank Graham Memorial Lecture at Princeton, the Schumpeter Lecture of the European Economic Association, and the Walras-Bowley and Frisch Lectures.

He is a member of the Israeli Academy of Sciences and Humanities, a Foreign Honorary Member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, a Corresponding Fellow of the British Academy, and a Distinguished Fellow of the American Economic Association. He served as President of both the Israeli Economic Association and the Econometric Society. His awards include a Honorary Doctorate from the Catholic University of Louvain, the Mahalanobis Memorial Medal, the Bernhard Harms Prize, the Rothschild Prize, the EMET Prize, the Nemmers Prize, the Onassis Prize, the Israel Prize, and the BBVA Frontiers of Knowledge Award.
