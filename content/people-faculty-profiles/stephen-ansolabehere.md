## Stephen Ansolabehere

**Frank G. Thompson Professor of Government**

**Email:** [sda@gov.harvard.edu](mailto:sda@gov.harvard.edu)

Stephen Ansolabehere is Frank G. Thompson Professor of Government at Harvard University. He specializes in public opinion and elections, with extensive publications on elections, mass media, representation, political economy, and public opinion (particularly regarding energy and the environment).

He authored five books: *Cheap and Clean*, *The Media Game*, *Going Negative*, *American Government*, and *The End of Inequality*.

His honors include being a Carnegie Scholar (2000), a Hoover National Fellow (1994), and a Truman Scholar (1982). He was inducted into the American Academy of Arts and Sciences in 2007.

He directed the Caltech/MIT Voting Technology Project from its founding in 2000 through 2004, serves on the Board of Overseers of the Reuters Institute of Journalism at Oxford University, and consults for CBS News Election Decision Desk. He directs the Center for American Political Studies at Harvard and is principal investigator of the Cooperative Congressional Election Study, described as "a collaborative effort of over 60 universities and colleges in the United States."
