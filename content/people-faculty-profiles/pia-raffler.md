## Pia Raffler

**Assistant Professor of Government**

Pia Raffler is an Assistant Professor in Political Science at Harvard University. She studies the politics of development, focusing on "governance, bureaucracy, and electoral politics in Sub-Saharan Africa." Her research examines political oversight of bureaucrats and its implications for public service provision, as well as electoral behavior. She "works closely with government agencies to understand impediments to accountable representation, often through large-scale field experiments." She holds a PhD from Yale University and a Master from Columbia University and is an EGAP member.
