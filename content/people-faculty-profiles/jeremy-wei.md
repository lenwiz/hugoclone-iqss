## Jeremy Wei

**Program Instructor, DCE**

Jeremy Wei has extensive experience in research methodologies, ethics, and data analysis, having facilitated various research projects at New York University and UTSC. His research "employs advanced statistical techniques and machine learning to examine complex relationships among variables." His current work explores AI and data privacy connections, resistance to AI adoption, and how reflective practices affect users' intentions to adopt AI technologies. He "assists organizations in transforming disadvantages into strengths" and founded iDox.ai, described as a leading AI-driven data privacy management organization.
