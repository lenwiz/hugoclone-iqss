## Max Bazerman

**Jesse Isidor Straus Professor of Business Administration, Harvard Business School**

**Email:** [mbazerman@hbs.edu](mailto:mbazerman@hbs.edu)

Max H. Bazerman holds the Jesse Isidor Straus Professorship of Business Administration at Harvard Business School and serves as Co-Director of the Center for Public Leadership at Harvard Kennedy School.

His research centers on decision making, negotiation, and ethics. He has authored, co-authored, or co-edited twenty books, including *The Power of Noticing* (Simon and Schuster, 2014) and *Blind Spots* (with Ann Tenbrunsel, Princeton University Press, 2011), along with over 200 research articles and chapters. He serves on editorial boards of multiple journals including *American Behavioral Scientist*, *Journal of Management and Governance*, *Mind and Society*, and others.

From 2002-2011, he was "consistently named one of the top 40 authors, speakers, and teachers of management by Executive Excellence." He earned the "Teacher of the Year" designation from Kellogg School's Executive Masters Program. Notable honors include:

- 2003: Everett Mendelsohn Excellence in Mentoring Award from Harvard's Graduate School of Arts and Sciences
- 2006: Honorary doctorate from University of London (London Business School), the Kulp-Wright Book Award, and Life Achievement Award from Aspen Institute's Business and Society Program
- 2008: Named among Ethisphere's 100 Most Influential in Business Ethics; received the Distinguished Educator Award from the Academy of Management
- 2009: Wyss Award for doctoral student mentoring and Williams Award for teaching excellence at HBS
- 2014: Academy of Management Career Award for Scholarly Contributions to Management; named Harvard Kennedy School's Advisory of the Year

His former doctoral students hold positions at leading business schools including Kellogg, Wharton, Fuqua, Johnson School at Cornell, Carnegie-Mellon, Stanford, University of Chicago, Notre Dame, Columbia, and Harvard Business School.

His professional activities span consulting work with numerous major corporations and organizations, and his "consulting, teaching, and lecturing includes work in 30 countries."
