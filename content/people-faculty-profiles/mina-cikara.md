## Mina Cikara

**Assistant Professor, Department of Psychology**

Professor Cikara studies "how the mind, brain, and behavior change when the social context shifts from 'me and you' to 'us and them.'" Her primary focus is on how group membership, competition, and prejudice disrupt empathy and the ability to see others as human. She employs diverse methodological tools including "standard laboratory experiments, implicit and explicit behavioral measures, fMRI and psychophysiology" to investigate failures of empathy, dehumanization, and intergroup misunderstanding. She is also interested in behavioral outcomes of these processes, particularly "willingness to harm out-group members."

Her role is Assistant Professor in the Department of Psychology at Harvard University, and she is an Affiliated Faculty member at the Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS). Her lab is the Intergroup Neuroscience Laboratory.
