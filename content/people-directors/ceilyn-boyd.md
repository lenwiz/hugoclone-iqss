## Ceilyn Boyd

**Interim Director of Data Science and Product Research**

**Email:** [cboyd@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:cboyd@iq.harvard.edu)

Oversees software development process for the Dataverse Project, managing partnership and grant responsibilities. Previously established and led Harvard Library Research Data Services Program.

Prior technical career in software engineering and technical project management, specializing in scientific data visualization, computer graphics, and special effects. Former employers include SRI International, Jet Propulsion Laboratory, Pacific Title and Art Studio, Mitsubishi Electric, and BBN Corporation.

**Education:** B.A. in Linguistics (Stanford); M.A. in Anthropology (Brandeis); Ph.D. in Library and Information Science (Simmons University)
