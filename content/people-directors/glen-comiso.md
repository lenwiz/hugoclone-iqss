## Glen Comiso

**Executive Director, Human Flourishing Program**

**Email:** [gcomiso@fas.harvard.edu](mailto:gcomiso@fas.harvard.edu)

Glen oversees the Human Flourishing Program at IQSS, managing strategic vision, donor relations, partnerships, and operations. Previously served as Chief of Staff in MITs presidents office.

Also worked at Boston Consulting Group, led the Massachusetts Life Sciences Collaborative, and oversaw economic initiatives for the City of Boston.

**Education:** MBA, Wharton; BS in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, UC Berkeley
