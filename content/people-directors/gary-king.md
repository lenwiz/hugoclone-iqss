## Gary King

**Director of IQSS; Albert J. Weatherhead III University Professor**

**Email:** [king@harvard.edu](mailto:king@harvard.edu)

One of 22 Harvard University Professors (the university most distinguished faculty position). Develops and applies empirical methods in social science research, from statistical theory to practical application.

Elected Fellow of 6 honorary societies, including the National Academy of Sciences (2010) and American Academy of Arts and Sciences (1998). Senior Science Advisor to WHO (1998-2003); Guggenheim Fellow (1994-1995). Over 125 journal articles, 15 open source software packages, and 8 books. Listed as the most cited political scientist of his cohort.

Co-founder of Crimson Hexagon (automated text analysis). Work on legislative redistricting used in most American states and by the U.S. Supreme Court. Led evaluation of Mexicos universal health insurance program.

**Education:** B.A., SUNY New Paltz (1980); Ph.D., University of Wisconsin-Madison (1984)

**Website:** [gking.harvard.edu](http://gking.harvard.edu/)
