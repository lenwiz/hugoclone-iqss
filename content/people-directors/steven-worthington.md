## Steven Worthington

**Director of Data Science Services**

**Email:** [sworthington@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:sworthington@iq.harvard.edu)

Steve has worked at IQSS since 2011. He earned a PhD in Biological Anthropology from New York University and is working on a master's in data science at Harvard. He hails from northern England, but has lived in New York and Helsinki, Finland. During his graduate studies, he developed an interest in computational biology and statistical programming using R and Python. His expertise spans Bayesian and frequentist inference, deep learning, phylogenetic methods, and geometric morphometrics. In his free time, he enjoys playing with his dog, Beckett, and indulging his interest in photography.
