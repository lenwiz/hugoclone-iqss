## Rachel Franklin

**Executive Director, Center for Geographic Analysis**

**Email:** [rachel_franklin@cga.harvard.edu](mailto:rachel_franklin@cga.harvard.edu)

Dr. Franklin joined the CGA in March 2025 with over 20 years experience in GIS, spatial analysis, and quantitative methods. Her research focuses on sources and impacts of demographic change at multiple spatial scales.

Previously a full professor at Newcastle University, she also holds roles at the Alan Turing Institute and Brown University. She began her career in 2002 at the US Census Bureau.

**Education:** PhD in Geography, University of Arizona (2004)
