## Jason Nemirow

**Director, Program on Higher Education Research**

**Email:** [nemirow@fas.harvard.edu](mailto:nemirow@fas.harvard.edu)

Leads PHERs strategic development and research agenda at IQSS and oversees the Council for Academic Freedom at Harvard (CAFH), a 200+ member faculty association.

Earned his PhD in Experimental Cognitive Psychology at Harvard, researching how people evaluate altruism. NSF Graduate Research Fellow.

**Education:** PhD, Harvard; B.A. summa cum laude in Biopsychology, UC Santa Barbara
