## Len Wisniewski

**Senior Director of Research Computing**

**Email:** [lwisniewski@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:lwisniewski@iq.harvard.edu)

Before IQSS, served as Engineering Manager for High Performance Computing at Sun Microsystems. Earlier roles at Acopia Networks (acquired by F5) and Thinking Machines (acquired by Sun). Focus on algorithms and file systems for parallel and distributed computers.

Teaches computer science and software security at Northeasterns Cybersecurity Masters program.

**Education:** B.A. in Mathematics and Computer Science, LaSalle University; Ph.D. in Computer Science, Dartmouth College
