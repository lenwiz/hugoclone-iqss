## Angie Acquatella

<strong>Visitor</strong>

Angie is an Assistant Professor at the Toulouse School of Economics who received her PhD in Economics from Harvard University in 2021. Her research focuses on the optimal design of health care policy, specifically public health insurance systems and pharmaceutical payment policy. She pursues policy designs that advance health equity, minimize risk for disadvantaged individuals, and incentivize socially valuable investments. Her work combines methods from optimal tax theory with traditional cost-effectiveness analysis in health economics, situated at the intersection of health economics and public finance.
