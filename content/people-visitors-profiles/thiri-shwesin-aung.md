## Thiri Shwesin Aung

<strong>Research Associate, TH Chan School</strong>

Thiri is an accomplished environmental scientist specializing in Land Cover and Land-Use Change (LCLUC) and energy systems. Her expertise spans "remote sensing, ecology, biogeochemistry, and land change modeling." Her current research focuses on "advancing methods, models, and emerging technologies in data science" for processing and analyzing heterogeneous remote sensing data, with applications to Earth System Science, especially in "data-scarce conflict regions." She uses geospatial science to "capture, analyze, and predict the intricacies of LCLUC at various scales," enabling understanding of environmental and societal impacts through land system analysis. She has extensive experience investigating "the impacts of armed conflict on land use, cropland abandonment, and socioeconomics" using remote sensing and modeling approaches. Her work appears in over 20 peer-reviewed journals. Thiri leads the CEFWG, an organization using geography and remote sensing to connect environmental condition, land use, and climate change with "processes of conflict, displacement, and resilience." Her research has received funding from the European Union, the World Bank, and the Norwegian Ministry of Foreign Affairs. She currently focuses on developing methods to "harness satellite data and artificial intelligence" to investigate human rights violations and environmental crimes during conflict and resource exploration.
