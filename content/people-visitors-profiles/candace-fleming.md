## Candace Fleming

<strong>IQSS Startup Foundry Executive Fellow</strong>

Candace Fleming is an Executive Fellow in the Harvard IQSS Startup Foundry, working "at the intersection of data, innovation, and entrepreneurship." She has over twenty years of experience as a finance and operations leader, helping build and scale companies by transforming complex ideas into practical, high-impact businesses.

She has served as CEO and COO across multiple ventures, including co-founding Crimson Hexagon with Gary King, leading fundraising, operational strategy, and team development efforts. Her work centers on data-driven decision-making and execution, especially in early-stage environments.

At IQSS, she partners with Harvard faculty to translate research into commercial applications. As part of the Startup Foundry, she contributes to venture creation and development, helping shape strategy, evaluate commercialization pathways, and build operational and financial foundations for bringing research-driven innovations to market. Her work spans Harvard's interdisciplinary ecosystem, engaging with faculty across multiple schools to advance ideas with societal impact.

She holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and degrees in Industrial Engineering and English from Stanford University.
