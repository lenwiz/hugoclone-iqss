## Leopoldo Biffi

<strong>PhD Candidate, Geneva Graduate Institute</strong>

Leopoldo Biffi is a PhD candidate in international relations/political science at the Graduate Institute in Geneva. He was previously a visiting fellow in the Department of Government at Harvard University. His research interests include international institutions, international trade and investment, the European Union, and Japan. His work focuses on the institutional design of international trade and investment agreements, and more recently on treaty implementation. He holds a BA in International Relations from King's College London (Department of War Studies) and an MA in International Relations/Political Science from the Graduate Institute in Geneva.
