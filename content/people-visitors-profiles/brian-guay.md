## Brian Guay

<strong>Postdoctoral Researcher, MIT</strong>

Brian Guay is a postdoctoral researcher at MIT in the Department of Political Science & Sloan School of Management. His research sits "at the intersection of political behavior and methodology," focusing specifically on "misinformation and polarization." His work draws on cognitive and social psychology theories and employs methods including "field experiments, surveys, and natural language processing with social media data." He earned his PhD from Duke University's Department of Political Science in spring 2021, where he participated in "the Duke Polarization Lab and Duke Initiative on Survey Methodology."
