## Jonathan D Teubner

<strong>Visiting Scholar, Human Flourishing Program</strong>

Jonathan D. Teubner is a Visiting Scholar at Harvard's Human Flourishing Program and a Fellow at the Institute for Advanced Studies in Culture at UVA. He has led initiatives in academia, business, and nonprofits, and pioneered the application of machine learning to improving peace building methods.

From 2021-2024, he led Social Connectedness Research at Harvard's IQSS. Previously, he was Associate Director of the Initiative on Religion, Politics and Conflict at UVA, where he led a team developing NLP tools. He founded FilterLabs.AI, a data analytics company tracking the impact of global propaganda on local communities, and directed Global Covenant Partners (2016-2019), an NGO focused on reducing religion-related violence.

He holds a Master's from Yale, a Ph.D. from Cambridge, and has held fellowships at Harvard, Yale, Sorbonne, and Humboldt University in Berlin.
