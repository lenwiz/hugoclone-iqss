## Illai Gescheit

**Executive Affiliate, Startup Foundry**

Illai is an Entrepreneur, Senior Advisor, Investor and Lecturer. He is a Founding Partner of Gescheit & Partners, a multi-stage venture advisory firm and a Venture Partner in a few venture funds. He serves as an Affiliate Lecturer teaching Strategy and Entrepreneurship at the London Business School and an Executive Affiliate at the Institute of Quantitative Social Sciences at Harvard University. He previously served as Venture Partner at Siemens Energy Ventures and as Entrepreneur-in-Residence at BP. He is an inventor of over 20 patents, and his early entrepreneurial story was featured in the Harvard-MIT book publication *Entrepreneurial Negotiation*. He is described as "a Top Voice on Linkedin sharing insights and thought leadership content." He is a member of the Ashoka Entrepreneur-to-Entrepreneur Network and a member of the Expert Network for the World Economic Forum.
