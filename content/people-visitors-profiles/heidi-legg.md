## Heidi R Legg

<strong>IQSS Affiliate; Former Fellow, Future of Media Project</strong>

Heidi Legg was the Future of Media fellow at IQSS at Harvard (2020-2022), where she indexed the entire US and Canadian media system with extensive ownership details. She mapped commercial and non-profit media ecosystems to offer solutions on transparency and trust in news. Her work appeared in Axios, USA Today, Nieman Labs, CJR, and Press-Gazette.

Previously at the Shorenstein Center (2018-2020), she wrote white papers on disinformation, local news, and thought leader magazines. She founded TheEditorial.com and published op-eds for CNN and the Boston Globe. She is mission driven to ensure more people have access to quality knowledge.

She holds a Graduate degree in Journalism from Concordia University (1994) and a Bachelor of Communications from Mount Saint Vincent University (1993).
