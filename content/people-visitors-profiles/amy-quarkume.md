## Amy Yeboah Quarkume

<strong>Associate of IQSS; Assoc Professor, Howard University</strong>

Dr. Amy Yeboah Quarkume, known as "Dr. A," is described as "a daughter of Africa, scholar, filmmaker, data scientist" and Associate Professor of Africana Studies at Howard University. She holds a PhD in African American Studies, two Master of Arts degrees in Sociology and African American Studies, and is pursuing an MS in Data Analytics and Computational Social Science from UMass, along with data science credentials from Harvard Extension School and MIT.

Her fellowships include the Andrew Mellon New Direction Fellowship, Mellon Just Futures Initiative partnership, Brown University Born-Digital Scholarly Publishing scholar designation, NCAR Innovator Fellow, and "White House Initiative HBCU All-Star Campus Mentor."

Her data science work "centers around AI Bias, data inequality and environmental justice," and she "employs an Africana Studies framework to examine the intersections of race and technology." She currently serves as Director of Graduate Studies for Applied Data Science in the Center for Applied Data Science and Analytics (CADSA), advancing Howard University's efforts in data science social justice research and training.
