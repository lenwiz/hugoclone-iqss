## Mike Reekie

**Senior Project Manager for Engineering**

**Email:** [mreekie@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:mreekie@iq.harvard.edu)

Mike Reekie is the Senior Project Manager for Engineering and Technical Operations at IQSS, Harvard. He handles day-to-day project management for the IT Operations team and Engineering software development projects for the DevOps team.

His background includes serving as Director of Systems Engineering at Momentum Telecom, where his team modernized and scaled nationwide infrastructure supporting data-over-cable and wholesale VoIP networks, managing provisioning for 900K cable modems for 300 independent cable ISPs.

He spent a significant portion of his career at Cisco Systems, starting in the data-over-cable field supporting cable modem and VoIP provisioning for large cable ISP and enterprise customers. His work focused on solving business problems with protocol servers, performing nationwide rollouts and upgrades of provisioning software/hardware, handling high-profile incident management, and authoring public-facing Root Cause responses.

He is most proud of two accomplishments: creating a training program for the Cisco TAC team—a three-week event that ran four years with U.S. and international participants covering protocols and advanced provisioning server features—and co-creating a 50+ machine performance and capacity test bed. This test bed automated complex scalability and capacity tests involving multiple server types communicating via DHCP, DNS, DDNS, LDAP, and DHCP Failover protocols, enabling identification of system-level bottlenecks.

His last role at Cisco was among the first 50 subject matter experts on Cisco Unified Computing Servers (UCS), where he enabled adoption and operationalization at critical customers, contributed to the body of knowledge for operating UCS at scale, and helped standardize engagement deliverables through automation.
