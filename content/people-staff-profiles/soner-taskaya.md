## Soner Taskaya

**Associate Director of IT Client Services**

**Email:** [staskaya@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:staskaya@iq.harvard.edu)

Soner Taskaya is Associate Director, IT Client Services at IQSS. He joined HMDC/IQSS as an IT Support Specialist in August 2003. Previously, he served as a Junior Systems Administrator at a startup software company for four years and worked in IT support at Harvard Medical School as a Client Support Representative for two years. He earned his B.A. in Business Management and Marketing from Curry College. In his free time, he enjoys traveling, hiking with his dog, snowboarding in winter, and golfing and fishing in summer.
