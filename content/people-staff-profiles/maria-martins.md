## Maria Martins

**Executive Assistant**

**Email:** [mmartins@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:mmartins@iq.harvard.edu)

Maria joined IQSS in March 2016. She previously served as an Executive Assistant in Harvard's Office for Sponsored Programs (OSP) for eight years. Before Harvard, she worked at The Inn at Harvard as Office Manager and at the Hilton Back Bay and Hilton Logan Airport as a Sales and Catering Assistant. She holds a "Bachelors of Science in Business Administration from Newbury College." In her free time, she "enjoys spending time with her family, running, and volunteering."
