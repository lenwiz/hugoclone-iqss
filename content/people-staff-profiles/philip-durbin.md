## Philip Durbin

**Software Developer, Dataverse Project**

**Email:** [philip_durbin@harvard.edu](mailto:philip_durbin@harvard.edu)

Phil Durbin holds a Psychology degree from the University of Dayton and works as a Software Developer on the Dataverse Project at IQSS. He was part of the team that rewrote Dataverse to be more inclusive of all science. He is enthusiastic about the growth of Dataverse installations to over a hundred, and actively participates in the community through the mailing list, chat room, and monthly community calls. He sends out the monthly Dataverse Community News and looks forward to the annual Dataverse Community Meeting. He is passionate about open source (having given a talk at JavaOne about running an open source project) and created a dataset called "Open Source at Harvard" in Harvard Dataverse. He co-founded the codecraft mailing list, has a project called Crossroads, and makes mobile games with his kids. He blogs at blogs.harvard.edu/pdurbin and his personal website is greptilian.com.
