## Lisa Galvin

**Academic Affairs Coordinator**

**Email:** [lgalvin@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:lgalvin@iq.harvard.edu)

Lisa joined IQSS in November 2011. Prior to this, Lisa worked as a temporary Human Resources Coordinator in Harvard's Faculty of Arts and Sciences (FAS) HR department. Lisa earned her Professional in Human Resources (PHR) certification in June 2013 and also holds a Bachelor's degree. She enjoys reading, television, movies, music, and keeping up with current events. Lisa lives with her husband in Cambridge.
