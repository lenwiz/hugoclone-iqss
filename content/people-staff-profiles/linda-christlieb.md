## Linda Christlieb

**Finance Associate**

**Email:** [lchristlieb@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:lchristlieb@iq.harvard.edu)

Linda has been with Harvard University since 2012. Prior to joining IQSS in 2015 in the Finance and Administration Division, Linda worked as a Faculty Assistant and Executive Assistant for an economics professor for three years. Her current responsibilities include payroll, processing payments and reimbursements and many other finance transactions for IQSS and it's affiliates. Linda holds a Bachelor's degree from Southern Connecticut State University and has two sons in college.
