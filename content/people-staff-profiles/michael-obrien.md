## Michael O'Brien

**Academic Affairs Coordinator**

**Email:** [mobrien@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:mobrien@iq.harvard.edu)

Michael joined IQSS in May 2024 as an Academic Affairs & HR Coordinator, having previously served as interim Office Manager in the IQSS' Business Operations Division. He provides academic personnel and staff support for all IQSS faculty affiliates across the University, focusing on academic policy advisement, classification and compensation, recruitment, Visa administration, leaves administration, and general HR policies. His prior experience includes public health consultancy at John Snow Inc., healthcare administration at Boston Children's Hospital, and operations at BXP. He "graduated summa cum laude from the University of Massachusetts Boston" with a BA in Sociology.
