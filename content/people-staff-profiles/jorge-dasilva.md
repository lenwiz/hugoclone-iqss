## Jorge Dasilva

**Senior Sponsored Research Administrator**

**Email:** [jdasilva@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:jdasilva@iq.harvard.edu)

Jorge joined the Sponsored Finance team at IQSS in July 2023 as a Sr. Sponsored Research Administrator. He supports several principal investigators affiliated with IQSS. He is described as "a seasoned finance professional with over 25 years of experience in both education and the business sector." Jorge joined Harvard in 2003 as a Grants Manager at the New England Primate Research Center and subsequently held roles with the Department of Psychology and the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. In these positions he "managed complex financial reporting and analysis, lead business operations initiatives and assisted faculty and staff with portfolio management." He lives with his wife and 3 children (15-year-old boy/girl twins and 14-year-old son) and in his spare time enjoys spending time on his boat and attending his children's sporting activities.
