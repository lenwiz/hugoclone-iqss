## Joshua Cetron

**Data Science Specialist**

**Email:** [jcetron@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:jcetron@iq.harvard.edu)

Josh is a data scientist, social scientist, and methodologist with a background in psychology, neuroscience, and multivariate statistics. As a Data Science Specialist at IQSS, he provides methods and analysis consulting services and collaborative support to Harvard-affiliate researchers across the sciences, focusing on experimental methods, exploratory analysis, and regression modeling of empirical data in R and python. He joined IQSS in June 2023 after completing his Ph.D. in Psychology at Harvard, where he studied psychological and neural representations of social and political information, including political propositional knowledge and social group attitudes. During graduate school, he developed an interest in methods and analysis development and support as a Teaching Fellow for multiple graduate-level statistics courses, teaching advanced statistical methods and collaborating with statisticians to develop new analytical methods. Prior to his Ph.D., he completed his B.A. in neuroscience at Dartmouth College, where he studied neural representations of concept knowledge acquired through classroom-based learning. Outside of work, he enjoys playing music, rock climbing, and exploring coffee shops in Somerville and Cambridge.
