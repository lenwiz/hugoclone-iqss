## Brendan Case

**Associate Director for Research, Human Flourishing Program**

**Email:** [brendan_case@fas.harvard.edu](mailto:brendan_case@fas.harvard.edu)

Dr. Case's research bridges the humanities and social sciences to explore the nature, distribution, and determinants of a flourishing life, with a particular focus on dispositions such as love, and on key institutional pathways to flourishing, such as religious communities and marriage.
