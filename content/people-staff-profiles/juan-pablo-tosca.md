## Juan Pablo Tosca

**Senior Software Developer, Dataverse**

**Email:** [juan_tosca@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:juan_tosca@iq.harvard.edu)

Juan Pablo Tosca is a Senior Software Developer who joined IQSS in 2023 to work on the Dataverse project. He previously worked at the Harvard Graduate School of Education as a Senior Software Developer focused on Java and Spring applications. He became involved with Dataverse and the Containerization Working Group before officially joining the team, and has contributed to Dataverse releases since version 6.1. He is originally from Xochimilco (meaning "place of the flowers" in Nahuatl) in Mexico City, and came to the United States in 2014 after completing his MBA at Universidad del Valle de Mexico. He is fluent in English and Spanish. In his free time, he enjoys discussing immigration, music, and technology.
