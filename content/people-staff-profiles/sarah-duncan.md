## Sarah Duncan

**IT Operations Manager, IQSS/Harvard-MIT Data Center**

**Email:** [slduncan@fas.harvard.edu](mailto:slduncan@fas.harvard.edu)

Sarah Duncan is the IT Operations Manager at IQSS/Harvard-MIT Data Center. She supports Social Science research using Linux and holds an undergraduate degree in Engineering and Applied Science and a Masters in Divinity, both from Harvard. Previously, she served as webweaver for Harvard's Department of Genetics at the Medical School, spent eleven and a half years handling IT for the Harrison/Wiley x-ray crystallography labs in Biochemistry, and most recently worked eight years at WGBH as a UNIX, Storage, and Windows Systems Administrator. She lives in Boston's North End with her wife Reay and two cats, speaks Spanish and Tamil (learned during a college year abroad in Madurai, India), and enjoys reading, learning new things, travel, cooking, yoga, crafts, and glass bead making and fusing.
