## Keri Lemasters

**Senior Sponsored Research Administrator**

**Email:** [klemasters@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:klemasters@iq.harvard.edu)

Keri joined the sponsored finance team at IQSS in February of 2019 and has worked in sponsored administration for nine years. She supports several principal investigators affiliated with IQSS in pre-award and post-award services for federal and private foundation awards. Prior to IQSS, she worked for five years as a Financial Analyst with the Office for Sponsored Programs at Harvard. At OSP, she was a key contributor in managing the Financial Reporting Application Portal (FRAP), which "drastically increased monthly automation of financial reports and invoices to over 60%." In 2016, she assisted HUIT/FSS with the FIRST project, which successfully transitioned FRAP automation into GMAS in April 2018, enabling new applications for sponsored compliance and financial reporting (Transaction monitoring and Financial Dashboard). She also contributed to university-wide financial award set up and revisions of thousands of funded projects feeding into Harvard's peripheral financial systems. She "was nominated as a Harvard Hero for remarkable sacrifice for co-workers, departments, and the University in 2015." She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Interdisciplinary Humanities from Michigan State University.
