## Jeffrey Blossom

**GIS Service Manager, Center for Geographic Analysis**

Jeffrey Blossom is the GIS Service Manager at the Center for Geographic Analysis (CGA). He provides expert guidance and support to researchers utilizing geographic information systems and spatial analysis methodologies in their work.
