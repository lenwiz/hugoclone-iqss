## Gabrielle Stone

**Events Coordinator**

**Email:** [gstone@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:gstone@iq.harvard.edu)

Gabrielle is a professional event planner who has worked with Harvard University for 10 years. She graduated from the University of Connecticut with a Bachelor's degree in English and has a Certificate of Special Studies in Administration and Management from Harvard Extension School, focusing on nonprofit management, marketing and public relations, museum administration, and fundraising. Her previous career was in publishing.
