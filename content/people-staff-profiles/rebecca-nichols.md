## Rebecca Nichols

**Program/Project Manager**

Rebecca Nichols is a results-driven program/project manager at Harvard's Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS), where she "helps advance interdisciplinary research and innovation" to address complex societal challenges. She brings extensive experience managing technical, research, and administrative projects across academic, nonprofit, and Fortune 500 settings, specializing in connecting diverse teams and turning ambitious ideas into operational successes.

At IQSS, she leads initiatives in program development, research facilitation, and process optimization. She is proficient in both agile and waterfall project management and excels at coordinating university resources, launching programs, and supporting tools and services that promote collaborative, data-driven social science. She is recognized for her collaborative style—bringing together faculty, administrators, developers, and researchers to build community, create efficiencies, and enhance IQSS impact.

She holds a Bachelor's degree in Psychology from Endicott College and brings strengths in instructional design, stakeholder engagement, and workflow improvement, with a continuous focus on leveraging tools and technologies to support learning, research, and organizational goals.

Outside work, she is passionate about hiking, kayaking, and photography. She enjoys immersing herself in new cultures through travel and is dedicated to mission work. She has "a special fondness for Peru, having visited three times" and developed meaningful connections through mission experiences there.
