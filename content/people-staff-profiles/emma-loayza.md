## Emma Loayza

**Office Manager**

**Email:** [egloayza@iq.harvard.edu](mailto:egloayza@iq.harvard.edu)

Emma joined IQSS in May of 2024. Prior to that, she worked as an Office Manager in both the Library Student Employment Program and the Digital Ventures Lab at Boston University Libraries. Her current work at IQSS focuses on physical space management, as well as assisting with a variety of projects for the IQSS Operations team. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Archaeology from Boston University, and enjoys reading, writing, and cooking in her free time.
