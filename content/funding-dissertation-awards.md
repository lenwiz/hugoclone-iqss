The Henry A. Murray Research Archive offers the Jeanne Humphrey Block Dissertation Award to a qualified Ph.D. candidate in the social sciences each spring.

## Key Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | Harvard PhD candidate (3rd year or above) in the social sciences, not graduating this year |
| **Amount** | $2,500 |
| **Deadline** | Currently on hold |

## How to Apply

Submit via [CARAT](https://carat.fas.harvard.edu/applicant/newApplication?applicationPurposeId=4&fundingSourceId=7268). Applicants must submit:

1. An abstract/description of the project (maximum 2 pages), identifying which study will be used and significance to the social sciences
2. Letter of recommendation from dissertation advisor
3. CV/Resume
4. Project budget

## Requirements for Recipients

- Recipient must archive final data and dissertation on the [Harvard Dataverse Repository](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/)
- Awardee will submit monthly updates on grant progress and confirm when resulting research project has been deposited

## Decision Criteria

Award decisions are based on:

- Significance to social science research
- Innovation
- Investigator evaluation
