## Transforming Social Science Research Into World-Changing Companies

**The IQSS Startup Foundry helps Harvard faculty transform social science insights into scalable technology companies that solve global challenges.**

## The Untapped Opportunity

The social sciences are a massive, underexplored innovation pipeline. Quantitative methods have improved and analytical capabilities have expanded, yet commercial applications remain largely untapped. Most researchers are unaware their insights could become transformative companies, creating opportunities for both academics and business.

Combining business expertise with academic discoveries can generate ventures contributing to healthcare, education, workplace productivity, and media:

- Academia gains real-world datasets, resources, and expertise
- Industry gains companies with defensible competitive advantages rooted in scientific discovery
- The result: substantial public good and commercial value

## Creating New Ventures

The IQSS Startup Foundry fosters collaborations through an ecosystem and venture creation resources. This is where it begins.
