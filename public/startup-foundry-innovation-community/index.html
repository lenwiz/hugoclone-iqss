The Innovation Community

The IQSS Startup Foundry helps Harvard social scientists turn research insights into viable startups, supporting early-stage software and AI ventures with commercialization typically taking 6-18 months depending on industry complexity, regulation, and development stage.

The program guides academic founders and business drivers through customer discovery, market research, MVP development, pilot customer programs, go-to-market plans, and fundraising. After spinning out from Harvard, operating founders stay connected as alumni while academic founders typically remain at Harvard as company advisors.

We have built a supportive, networked innovation ecosystem that produces sustainable ventures addressing global challenges.

Deep Experience, Proven Results

IQSS has extensive experience transforming academic projects into commercial startups, nonprofits, open-source initiatives, and research centers at Harvard and globally. Their work has amplified social science research, created thousands of jobs, and generated hundreds of millions in value.

Recent examples include:

  • An AI drug discovery company that raised a significant pre-seed round
  • A platform clinically proven to reduce maternal mortality across hundreds of hospitals
  • An AI-driven SaaS platform for industrial engineering that completed Y-Combinator
  • Two innovative healthcare companies in fertility
  • A startup using AI for context-aware human memory assistance that completed a multi-million dollar seed round

Join Our Innovation Community!

The Foundry brings together a curated network of business leaders, investors, and industry experts who share the vision of translating breakthrough research into transformative ventures.

1. Advisory Board Members

Distinguished investors, entrepreneurs, and academics who advise IQSS leadership on strategic direction. They guide key initiatives, provide high-level guidance to founders, and support IQSS within their networks. Members get early access to cutting-edge innovations.

Time commitment: Quarterly board meetings (2-3 hours each), plus optional one-on-one meetings with founders.

Meet our Board of Advisors

2. Executive Affiliates

Accomplished entrepreneurs and business leaders who serve as exclusive mentors to the most promising startups. They commit to year-long partnerships providing personalized guidance and strategic introductions. They receive official Harvard appointments.

Time commitment: Minimum 10 hours per month working with founders.

Meet our Executive Affiliates

3. Business Drivers

Professionals who work hands-on with faculty to transform academic research into viable companies. They develop business models, validate markets, perform customer discovery, and iterate with faculty. This often results in launching the startup and a leadership role.

Time commitment: Months to a year or more; significant commitment required.

4. Industry Experts

Specialists who contribute knowledge to solve challenges founders encounter across diverse sectors. The Foundry reaches out with specific asks when needed.

Time commitment: 1-3 hours per request; may evolve into longer-term mentorship.

5. Foundry Supporters and Philanthropists

Visionary philanthropic funders who make the program possible through tax-deductible gifts and business advice. They help build programming, structure, and support connecting academic and commercial worlds.

6. Early-Stage Investors

Provide startup capital and guidance to emerging ventures in exchange for equity. These investors are aligned with the Foundry but external to it.

While many key positions are filled by invitation through a selective review process, we actively seek dedicated volunteers and encourage interested individuals to contact us about opportunities to contribute.
