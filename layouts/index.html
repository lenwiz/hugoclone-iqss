Our Impact

Investigating Flight Attendants' Safety

The longest-running longitudinal study of its kind, led by Dr. Eileen McNeely at IQSS. Laboratory tests found harmful substances on airline uniforms, connecting them to reports of debilitating medical issues. Findings provided evidence in a class action lawsuit holding the manufacturer accountable.
